Report an Issue

One form for everything: defects, cleanliness, security, noise, parking, provider performance. Urgent safety issues — call 115 (fire & rescue) or 999 (police) first, then log here for the record.

F6 — Complaint & incident form (MF-PS1-COP-005)

Fill it on screen (fillable PDF) or print and hand in at the office. Fields: your details · location (block/floor/lot) · category (defect / leak / cleanliness / security / noise / parking / provider performance / other) · urgency (safety hazard / urgent / standard) · description · list of attached documents (drawings, photos, PDFs).
Acknowledged within 2 working days with a reference number; safety hazards actioned the same day. Recurring issues feed the maintenance and KPI reviews — reporting here is the fastest way to get things fixed, and it protects you: written complaints are on the record.
